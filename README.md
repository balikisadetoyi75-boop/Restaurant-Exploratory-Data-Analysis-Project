# **RESTAURANT ORDERS & DELIVERY - EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS PROJECT**

## **Objective**
The objective of this project is to understand ordering patterns, restaurant performance, and customer experience across different restaurants within a specific area.

---

## **Datasets Used**
- **Orders** table
- **Restaurants** table

---

## **Tools Used**
- **SQL Server**

---

## **EDA Framework Used**
This analysis follows a structured exploratory data analysis framework:

1. **Database exploration**
2. **Dimension exploration**
3. **Date/time exploration**
4. **Measures exploration**
5. **Magnitude exploration**
6. **Ranking**

---

## **Key Questions Answered**
- What cuisines are available and how many restaurants belong to each cuisine?
- What zones exist and how are restaurants distributed across them?
- How many orders occur by hour of the day?
- What is the average order amount?
- Which restaurants generate the highest total order amount?
- Which cuisines contribute the most orders?

---

## **Key Insights**
- **Chinese and North Indian cuisines** are the most preferred and contribute the highest number of orders.
- **Zone D** has the highest restaurant population, while **Zone A** has the lowest.
- **Card payments** are the most preferred payment method, although a significant number of customers still choose **cash on delivery**.
- **Peak order time** occurs in the afternoon, with an **average delivery time of approximately 30 minutes**.
